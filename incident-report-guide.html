How to use it

Incident Report Builder

Instead of a blank template, this app interviews you — by typing or dictating — one question at a time, in the exact section order and skip logic of Alpha's official incident report.

Video walkthrough coming soon The Loom will drop in right here — the steps below work in the meantime.

Step by step

  1. Sign in with your @alpha.school Google accountAccess is restricted to Alpha staff.
  2. First visit: pick your campusThis sticks going forward — your reports are private to your own campus.
  3. Start a report and just talk through what happenedType or dictate — the app asks one question at a time, the same way a colleague filling out the real template would.
  4. Answer at your own paceYour draft auto-saves after every answer, so nothing is lost if you close the tab and come back later.
  5. Finish the interviewOnce every required section is covered (skip logic applies automatically — you won't be asked questions that don't apply to this incident).
  6. Download the finished reportTap "Download report (.doc)" — it's ready to file or drag straight into Google Drive.
Note: your reports are only visible to your own campus. Leadership accounts can see reports across every campus, but coordinators only ever see their own site's.
