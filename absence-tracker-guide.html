How to use it

Staff Absence Tracker

A multi-campus time-off request tool. Guides submit a request, their campus's Head Guide approves or denies it right from Slack, and an approved request lands automatically on the campus calendar — no manual entry.

Video walkthrough coming soon The Loom will drop in right here — the steps below work in the meantime.

For guides: requesting time off

  1. Sign in with your school Google accountRestricted to Alpha's allowed school domains.
  2. First time only: pick your campusThis sticks for every future visit — you won't be asked again.
  3. Submit your requestYour name, email, and campus are pulled from your signed-in account automatically — you can only submit for yourself.
  4. Your Head Guide gets a Slack DMWith the request details and Approve/Deny buttons right in the message.
  5. Check your status anytimeMy Requests shows a pending / approved / denied badge for everything you've submitted, including any reason given for a denial.

For Head Guides & coordinators

  1. Approve or deny right from SlackApprove creates the calendar event automatically on your campus's calendar; Deny asks for an optional reason first. Either way, the requester gets their own Slack DM with the outcome.
  2. Review everything on /adminSee every request for your campus, filterable by status, name, or date — plus a tool to add a past absence for backfilling history.
  3. Manage the campus directory (org admins only)Add new campuses, set each campus's calendar, and manage Head Guides — including who's primary and pausing someone's notifications without removing them.
Note: if your campus's Head Guide has notifications paused (or none is on file), the request automatically falls back to the next Head Guide, or a safety-net contact — a request is never silently lost.
