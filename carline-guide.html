How to use it

Parent Pickup & Dismissal

A live, color-coded pickup board — now rebuilt to support every Alpha campus from one shared site, each with its own roster, roster password, and dismissal settings.

Video walkthrough coming soon The Loom will drop in right here — the steps below work in the meantime.

Finding your campus

  1. Open the app and search for your campusThe home page is a searchable list of every campus that's been set up — type a few letters and tap yours to go straight to its board.
  2. Bookmark your campus's own linkOnce you're on your campus's board, the URL is unique to your site (e.g. .../your-campus) — save that link so staff can skip the search next time.

Running dismissal

  1. Check a student in from ScanType a name, scan their QR decal, or tap the mic and just say the name — voice works on Chrome, and typing is always the fallback if a browser doesn't support it.
  2. Watch the timer colorsGreen means just checked in, orange means about a minute waiting, red means two or more — handle red names first.
  3. Check them out from CheckoutSame options as Scan — type, scan, or use voice — with one tap to confirm.
  4. Use Exceptions for ride changesIf a student is riding home with a different family or another student's car today, mark it here so it isn't flagged as wrong.
  5. Look up Authorized Pickup on the spotSearch a student or family name to see exactly who's cleared to pick them up — no need to call the office to confirm.

Reports & roster

  1. Print a dismissal reportReports gives you a printable, dated pickup report for any day — handy for a paper record.
  2. Check Insights and the LeaderboardSee dismissal-time trends over time, and which staff have the fastest check-ins/checkouts.
  3. Manage your rosterRoster is behind its own password (separate from the main board) — add, edit, or bulk-import students via CSV. There's a step-by-step Roster Import Guide in the admin area if you're building that spreadsheet for the first time.
  4. Add a same-day guest ("shadow") studentFor a visiting or temporary student who isn't in your permanent roster — set how long they should stay listed, and they're removed automatically after that date.
  5. Adjust your campus's SettingsDismissal timing and other per-campus preferences live here.

For org admins: adding a new campus

  1. Sign in at the admin loginSeparate from every campus's own roster password — this is the org-wide super-admin account.
  2. Create the new campusGive it a name and a URL slug (e.g. "Denver" → .../denver), set its roster password, and add an admin email/password for that site.
Note: this is how any Alpha campus gets its own fully working instance — no separate deployment needed, just a new entry in the admin panel.
