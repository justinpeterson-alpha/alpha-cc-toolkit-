How to use it

Fire Drill Attendance

A real-time headcount tool for fire drills and real evacuations. Guides check in the students at the rally point, coordinators watch who's still unaccounted for, and every count is saved for review.

Video walkthrough coming soon The Loom will drop in right here — the steps below work in the meantime.

Step by step

  1. Tap "I'm a Guide" and select your nameFilling in for someone else? Just type your name in if it's not in the list.
  2. When the alarm goes (drill or real), check in your studentsSearch or say a student's name at the rally point to check them in — you can check in any student, not just your own.
  3. Watch for the colorGreen means checked in with you. Amber means another guide already has that student checked in — no need to double up.
  4. Coordinators: start the countTap "Start" to open the Dashboard, which shows a live Expected / Checked-In / Unaccounted breakdown, broken out by level and normal guide.
  5. Chase down anyone unaccountedThe Dashboard tells you exactly who's missing and who their usual guide is, so you know who to send looking.
  6. End the count when it's clearThis freezes the results and saves them to History for review afterward — useful for compliance records.
  7. Manage your roster anytimeCoordinators can add, edit, or remove students and guides right in the app — no redeploy needed.
Note: this app has no login screen so it's fast to use in an emergency — only share the link with staff, not publicly.
