How to use it

Ask Alpha

A verified answer library and AI-assisted Q&A page for Alpha staff — policy, security, devices, health & safety, and more. It only answers from vetted, sourced entries, never from general AI knowledge.

Video walkthrough coming soon The Loom will drop in right here — the steps below work in the meantime.

Step by step

  1. Sign in with your @alpha.school Google accountAccess is restricted to Alpha staff email domains.
  2. Pick your campusThis makes sure campus-specific answers (not just org-wide ones) surface for your site.
  3. Ask your questionType it in on the Ask page. It searches the verified answer library and only writes a response when there's real supporting material — it won't guess from general knowledge.
  4. Check the sourcesEvery answer lists which library entries it's based on, each dated, sourced, and owned by a real person.
  5. Nothing matched? Submit the question anywayIt routes to the right domain owner (security, facilities, health & safety, devices, etc.), who gets Slack-notified to add a verified answer.
  6. Flag anything that looks staleUse "Flag as outdated" on any answer — it notifies the domain owner to review and update it.
  7. Hit a rate limit?Ask is capped at 30 questions/hour/person. Use the Library page instead — plain keyword search, no AI, no limit.
Note: right now only Miami has real campus-specific content in the library — every other campus is empty. If you're at another site, submitting your own campus's answers as questions come up is the fastest way to fix that for everyone there.
