tools

Apps for the jobs that eat your day

Each tool solves one real campus problem. Live tools are ready to use; queued ones are next up.

live Carline A live, color-coded pickup board, built to run at every Alpha campus from one shared site. Check students in by QR decal, name, or voice, watch dismissal timers turn green → orange → red, look up authorized pickups, and print a dismissal report. Open app Guide
live Ask Alpha Ask questions on policy, security, devices, and safety and get answers pulled strictly from a verified, sourced answer library — never general AI knowledge. Open to all Alpha staff. Open app Guide
live Absence Tracker Guides request time off; their campus's Head Guide approves or denies right from a Slack DM, and an approved request lands automatically on that campus's Google Calendar. Open app Guide
live Incident Reports Talk through what happened — by typing or dictating — and the app interviews you one question at a time, following Alpha's official incident report template in order, then exports a ready-to-file .doc. Open app Guide
live Ops Requests Submit facilities, IT, or supply requests in one place — you get a Slack DM the moment one comes in, and taskers can check off open jobs from a public list with no login needed. Open app Guide
queued Fire Drill Attendance A live headcount app for fire drills and real evacuations — guides check students in, coordinators see who's unaccounted for. Built for Miami today; a multi-campus version (like Carline's) is planned.
queued Package Tracker Log deliveries on arrival and notify the right person so nothing sits in the office.
queued Supply & Inventory Track what's on hand, get notified when something's running low, and know what's already on order before you run out.

workflows

Automations that run in the background

Documented, repeatable workflows you can copy — the connective tissue between your tools and your team.

Slack Daily Briefing

Schedule→ Scan DMs & channels→ Post to my Slack DM
  • Reads yesterday's DMs and your key channels, separating urgent items from noise
  • Builds one prioritized to-do list, tagged by channel or DM, across everything that needs a response
  • Posts as a threaded message to your own Slack self-DM every morning — one reply per section, plus an optional Gmail mirror
Copy the setup

Gmail Daily Briefing

Schedule→ Scan inbox & sent→ Draft the briefing
  • Reads yesterday's inbox and sent mail for what actually needs a reply
  • Filters out newsletters and automated noise, keeping real deadlines and alerts
  • Leaves a ready-to-read draft in Gmail every morning — nothing sends on its own
